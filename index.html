Threat awareness

Understand the warning signs

Scammers create urgency, fear or curiosity so that you act before checking the facts.

01

Phishing

Fraudulent emails, messages or websites that impersonate trusted organizations to steal information.

  • Unexpected login or payment request
  • Misspelled or unrelated website address
  • Pressure to act immediately
  • Request for passwords, OTPs or card information
02

Digital-arrest scam

Criminals impersonate police, courts or government agencies and falsely accuse victims of crimes.

  • Threat of immediate arrest over a video call
  • Demand for secrecy or continuous surveillance
  • Request to transfer money to a "safe account"
  • Fake uniforms, documents or official-looking rooms
03

AI-generated fake news

Manipulated text, images, audio or video designed to imitate credible reporting or public figures.

  • No credible source or original context
  • Emotional headline that demands immediate sharing
  • Unnatural facial, voice or visual details
  • Claim not reported by other reliable outlets
